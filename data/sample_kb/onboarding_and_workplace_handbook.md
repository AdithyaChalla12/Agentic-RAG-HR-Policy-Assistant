# Lumen Labs Onboarding and Workplace Handbook

Owner: People Operations. Last reviewed: 2026-06-20.

## First week checklist

1. Day 1: Collect your laptop from IT (or receive it by courier if remote) and complete Okta setup.
2. Day 1: Complete the I-9 form in Workday. Bring identity documents to your first-day meeting or upload them if remote.
3. Day 2: Finish the mandatory Security Awareness and Code of Conduct trainings in Workday Learning.
4. Day 3: Enroll in benefits (see the Benefits and Expenses Guide; deadline is 30 days from start).
5. By Friday: Meet your onboarding buddy and set 30-60-90 day goals with your manager.

## Payroll

Employees are paid semi-monthly, on the 15th and the last business day of each month.
Pay stubs and W-2 forms are available in Workday under Pay. Direct deposit changes made before the 5th take effect in the next pay cycle.

## Hybrid work policy

- Hybrid employees work from the office on Tuesdays and Thursdays.
- Fully remote roles are marked as remote in the offer letter.
- Working from another country for more than 14 days in a year needs People Operations and Legal approval in advance, because of tax and immigration rules.

## Performance reviews

Formal reviews happen twice a year, in April and October. Each cycle includes a self-review, peer feedback from 2 to 4 colleagues, and a manager review. Compensation changes are decided in the April cycle only.

## Workplace conduct

Lumen Labs does not tolerate harassment, discrimination, or retaliation.
Report concerns to your manager, your HR Business Partner, or anonymously through the EthicsPoint hotline. Retaliating against anyone who reports a concern in good faith is grounds for termination.
