# Lumen Labs Leave and Time Off Policy

Owner: People Operations. Applies to all full-time US employees. Last reviewed: 2026-07-01.

## Paid time off (PTO)

Full-time employees accrue 1.67 days of PTO per month (20 days per year).
PTO accrual starts on the first day of employment. Unused PTO carries over up to a maximum of 5 days into the next calendar year; anything above 5 days expires on January 1.

### Requesting PTO

1. Submit the request in Workday under Time Off > Request Time Off.
2. Requests of 3 days or fewer need at least 5 business days of notice.
3. Requests longer than 3 days need at least 3 weeks of notice.
4. Your manager approves or declines within 2 business days. If there is no response, escalate to your HR Business Partner.

## Sick leave

Sick leave is separate from PTO. Employees receive 10 paid sick days per year, which do not carry over.
A doctor's note is required only for absences longer than 3 consecutive working days.
Notify your manager before your scheduled start time on the day you are out.

## Parental leave

All employees who have completed 90 days of employment are eligible for parental leave.

- Birthing parents: 16 weeks fully paid.
- Non-birthing parents (including adoptive and foster parents): 12 weeks fully paid.
- Leave can be taken in up to two blocks within 12 months of the birth or placement.
- Notify People Operations at least 30 days before the expected start date when possible.

## Bereavement leave

Up to 5 paid days for the loss of an immediate family member, and up to 2 paid days for extended family. No documentation is required.

## Company holidays

Lumen Labs observes 11 paid holidays per year, plus a company-wide winter break from December 24 through January 1. The holiday calendar is published in Workday every November.
