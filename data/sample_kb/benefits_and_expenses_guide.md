# Lumen Labs Benefits and Expenses Guide

Owner: People Operations and Finance. Last reviewed: 2026-08-15.

## Health insurance

Lumen Labs offers three medical plans through BlueShield: PPO Plus, HMO Standard, and HDHP with HSA.
The company pays 100% of employee premiums and 75% of dependent premiums on all plans.
For the HDHP, Lumen Labs contributes 1,000 USD per year to individual HSAs and 2,000 USD to family HSAs.

### Enrollment windows

- New hires must enroll within 30 days of their start date. Coverage begins on the first day of employment.
- Open enrollment runs every year from November 1 to November 15.
- Qualifying life events (marriage, birth, loss of other coverage) allow changes within 30 days of the event.

## 401(k) retirement plan

Employees can enroll in the 401(k) through Fidelity from day one.
Lumen Labs matches 100% of contributions up to 4% of base salary. The match vests immediately.

## Learning and development stipend

Each employee receives 1,500 USD per calendar year for courses, certifications, conferences, and books related to their role.
Submit the receipt in Expensify under the category "Learning" within 60 days of purchase. Unused stipend does not carry over.

## Home office stipend

New hires receive a one-time 500 USD home office stipend, plus 50 USD per month for internet, paid through payroll.

## Expense reimbursement

1. Submit expenses in Expensify within 30 days of the purchase date.
2. Attach an itemized receipt for every expense over 25 USD.
3. Your manager approves; Finance reimburses in the next payroll cycle.
4. Expenses submitted after 60 days are not reimbursed without CFO approval.

## Business travel

- Book flights and hotels through Navan so they are covered by the travel policy.
- Economy class for flights under 6 hours; premium economy allowed for flights of 6 hours or more.
- Hotel cap: 250 USD per night in standard cities, 350 USD per night in New York, San Francisco, London, and Tokyo.
- Meals while traveling: up to 75 USD per day.
